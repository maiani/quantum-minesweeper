Mine counter

The ⟨💣⟩ counter above the board: how many mines the board holds on average. The angle brackets are how physicists write an expectation value, an average over every way the board could turn out.

A mine in a superposition counts as a fraction until you measure it: a fifty-fifty cell adds 0.5. In Clear mode you win when the counter reaches zero, because then no cell can turn out to be a mine.
