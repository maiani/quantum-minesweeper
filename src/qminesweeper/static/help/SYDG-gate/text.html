The SYDG button applies $\sqrt{Y}$ backwards. Like $\sqrt{Y}$, it turns a definite cell into an even chance.

$$ |0\rangle \to |-\rangle \qquad |1\rangle \to |+\rangle $$

In the useful direction it sends $|+\rangle$ to a certainly safe cell, and it undoes anything $\sqrt{Y}$ did.

Formally, a 90$^\circ$ rotation about the "y-axis", in the opposite direction to $\sqrt{Y}$.
