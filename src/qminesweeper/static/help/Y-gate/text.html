Y gate

Also swaps mine and empty. Judged by the numbers on the board, Y does what X does.

$$ |0\rangle \to |1\rangle \qquad |1\rangle \to |0\rangle $$

The difference is a phase, which no clue can show you. It surfaces once the cell is in superposition or entangled: Y sends $|+\rangle \to |-\rangle$, where X leaves both alone.

Formally, a 180$^\circ$ rotation about the "y-axis", the axis through $|i\rangle$ and $|-i\rangle$.
