S gate

Like Z, the S gate changes no number on the board. It turns a cell's superposition without moving it towards mine or safe.

$$ |+\rangle \to |i\rangle \qquad |-\rangle \to |-i\rangle $$

$|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$ are left alone. S earns its keep as a set-up move before a gate that does change the odds: $\sqrt{X}$ turns $|i\rangle$ into a certainly safe cell.

Formally, a 90$^\circ$ rotation about the "z-axis". It is the "square root" of Z: applying it twice gives the same result as Z.
