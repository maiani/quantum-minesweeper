CX gate

Your first gate that acts on two cells at once. Click the control cell first, then the target.

The rule is simple: if the control holds a mine, flip the target; if it does not, leave the target alone.

$$ |0\rangle|0\rangle \to |0\rangle|0\rangle \qquad |1\rangle|0\rangle \to |1\rangle|1\rangle $$

The interesting part is what happens when the control has not decided. Apply CX with a control in an even superposition and neither cell ends up with an answer of its own — instead the two become entangled: measure one and the other is settled too, without either having been fixed beforehand. This is how the boards from Level 2 up are built.

CX undoes itself. Apply it twice with the same control and target and you are back where you started, which is what makes it your tool for pulling entangled cells apart again.
