X gate

Swaps mine and empty. A cell that certainly holds a mine becomes certainly safe, and a certainly safe cell becomes a mine.

$$ |0\rangle \to |1\rangle \qquad |1\rangle \to |0\rangle $$

On a cell with an even chance of holding a mine, X changes nothing you can see: the odds come out the same either way.

Formally, a 180$^\circ$ rotation about the "x-axis", the axis through $|+\rangle$ and $|-\rangle$. It is the quantum version of NOT.
