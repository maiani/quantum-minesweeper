Hadamard gate

H moves a cell between certainty and an even chance. A cell that is definitely safe or definitely a mine becomes a 50/50 cell, and a 50/50 cell becomes definite.

$$ |0\rangle \to |+\rangle \qquad |+\rangle \to |0\rangle $$

$$ |1\rangle \to |-\rangle \qquad |-\rangle \to |1\rangle $$

This is how a superposition gets defused: bring a cell to $|+\rangle$, apply H, and it is certainly safe. Land on $|-\rangle$ instead and H makes it a certain mine, so watch the clue numbers before you commit.

Formally, a 180$^\circ$ rotation about the axis halfway between x and z, $\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1,0,1)$. It exchanges the two axes, which is also why it is the usual first step in building entanglement.
