SWAP gate

Exchanges two cells completely. Whatever the first cell held — a mine, a safe square, a superposition, one half of an entangled pair — the second cell now holds it, and the first holds what the second had.

$$ |0\rangle|1\rangle \to |1\rangle|0\rangle $$

SWAP creates no entanglement of its own; it only moves what is already there. Use it to bring an awkward cell next to a clue you can read, or to carry one half of an entangled pair away from its partner.

Applying SWAP twice puts everything back.
