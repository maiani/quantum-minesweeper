Sandbox reveal

Reveal lets you peek at the quantum board in Sandbox without changing it. It does not open a cell or count as a move.

The red fill shows how likely a cell is to contain a mine. No red means it is safe, a half-filled cell has an equal chance of being safe or mined, and a fully red cell is certain to be a mine.

Leave Reveal open while you try different gates and watch the colours and lines change. Select Hide when you want the normal board back.
