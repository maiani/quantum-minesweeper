√X gate

The SX button applies $\sqrt{X}$ — half an X gate. Instead of flipping a cell all the way from safe to mine, it moves it halfway: a definite cell comes out an even chance.

$$ |0\rangle \to |-i\rangle \qquad |1\rangle \to |i\rangle $$

It works the other way too, which is the useful direction: applied to $|i\rangle$ it gives a certainly safe cell. Applying it twice is the same as applying X once.

Formally, a 90$^\circ$ rotation about the "x-axis", the axis through $|+\rangle$ and $|-\rangle$.
