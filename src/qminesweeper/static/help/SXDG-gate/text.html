√X† gate

The SXDG button applies $\sqrt{X}$ backwards. Like $\sqrt{X}$, it turns a definite cell into an even chance.

$$ |0\rangle \to |i\rangle \qquad |1\rangle \to |-i\rangle $$

In the useful direction it sends $|-i\rangle$ to a certainly safe cell, and it undoes anything $\sqrt{X}$ did.

Formally, a 90$^\circ$ rotation about the "x-axis", in the opposite direction to $\sqrt{X}$.
