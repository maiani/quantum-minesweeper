CY gate

Like CX, but with Y: if the control cell holds a mine, apply Y to the target; if it does not, leave the target alone. Click the control first.

$$ |0\rangle|0\rangle \to |0\rangle|0\rangle \qquad |1\rangle|0\rangle \to |1\rangle|1\rangle $$

Judged by the numbers on the board, CY does what CX does: it flips the target when the control is a mine, and entangles the two cells when the control is in superposition. The difference is the phase Y adds, which changes what later gates will do to the pair.

CY also undoes itself.
