Z gate

Z never changes a mine probability — not on the cell you apply it to, and not anywhere else on the board. Apply it on its own and no number moves.

$$ |+\rangle \to |-\rangle \qquad |-\rangle \to |+\rangle $$

What it does change is which superposition the cell is in, and that decides what the next gate will do. H turns $|+\rangle$ into a certainly safe cell, but turns $|-\rangle$ into a certain mine.

$|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$ are left completely alone; they are the eigenstates of Z. Formally, a 180$^\circ$ rotation about the "z-axis", the axis through $|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$.
