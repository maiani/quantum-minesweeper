Mine counter

The ⟨💣⟩ counter above the board: how many mines the board holds on average. The angle brackets are how physicists write an average taken over every way the board could turn out — an expectation value.

A cell in superposition counts as a fraction until it is measured, so a cell that is half likely to be a mine adds 0.5. The counter is rarely a whole number.

From Level 1 up you win when it reaches zero, because at that point no cell can turn out to be a mine.
