Entanglement counter

The counter with the two linked rings, measured in bits. It asks of every cell how entangled that cell is with the whole rest of the board, and adds the answers up.

One entangled pair — a Bell pair — adds 2 bits, one for each of its two cells. A cell that is not entangled adds 0, even when it is still uncertain whether it holds a mine: superposition on its own is not entanglement.

So this counter tells you how much entanglement is on the board, but not where it is. To find where, use the entanglement probe.
