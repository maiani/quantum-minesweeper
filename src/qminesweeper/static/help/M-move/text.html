Measure

Measuring a cell opens it and forces it to settle on one outcome: mine or no mine. If it settles on a mine, the game is over.

If it is safe, the cell stays open and shows a clue — how many mines to expect in the cells touching it. A neighbour that is half likely to be a mine adds 0.5, so clues are often fractions.

Measuring is the one move you cannot undo. An opened cell has settled for good, and gates no longer work on it.
